Our Story

We started with a
quiet conviction

Founded in 2019 in Bangalore, NovaSpark was built on one belief: that great technology, thoughtfully applied, changes everything. We were tired of seeing talented teams let down by slow agencies and bloated processes.

So we built the alternative — a studio that combines the rigour of a consultancy with the soul of a product team. We move fast, we think deeply, and we build things that endure.

Today, we're a 60-person team with offices across Bangalore, London, and Singapore, serving clients from seed stage to enterprise.
