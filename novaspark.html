Our Story

We started with one
bold belief

Founded in 2019 in Bangalore, NovaSpark was born from a single conviction: that every great business deserves great technology. We were tired of seeing talented teams held back by legacy systems and slow agencies.

So we built the antidote — a team of engineers, designers, and strategists who move fast, think big, and ship products that actually work.

Today, we're a 60+ person team serving clients across 18 countries, from seed-stage startups to Fortune 500 companies.
